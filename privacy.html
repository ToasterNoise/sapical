Privacy policy

Sapical desktop widgets · Last updated 6 November 2026

Sapical is a desktop application for Windows that runs entirely on your own computer. It has no servers, no accounts of its own, no analytics and no advertising.

What Sapical accesses

  • Google Calendar (only if you connect it): read-only access to your calendar list and events, using the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly. Sapical uses this solely to display your upcoming events in the calendar widget on your desktop.
  • Photos: image files in a folder you choose on your computer. They are read locally and never uploaded.
  • Weather: the location you enter is sent to the free Open-Meteo service (open-meteo.com) to fetch the forecast.

How your Google data is used and stored

  • Calendar data is fetched directly from Google to your computer and shown only there. It is not sent to the developer or to any third party, not sold, and not used for advertising or any purpose other than displaying your events.
  • Sapical stores a Google sign-in (refresh) token and a cache of recent events on your computer, encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI) so only your Windows account can read them.
  • Sapical's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

  • Choose Disconnect in Sapical's calendar menu. This deletes the stored token and event cache and revokes Sapical's access with Google.
  • You can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling Sapical and deleting the %APPDATA%\Sapical folder removes all of its local data.
