Sapical

Lightweight, native desktop widgets for Windows 11.

Sapical places three small widgets directly on the Windows desktop, behind your application windows:

  • Calendar: a month view and agenda of your Google Calendar events (read-only).
  • Photos: a slideshow of images from a folder on your computer.
  • Weather: current conditions and a 7-day forecast from Open-Meteo.

Google Calendar access

When you choose Connect Google Calendar, Sapical asks Google for read-only access to your calendar events so it can display them on your desktop. It cannot create, change or delete anything. Your events are shown only on your own computer; Sapical has no servers and sends your data nowhere else.
